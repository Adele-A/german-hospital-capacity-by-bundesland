## Notes on comparability

- **COVID-19:** 2020–2021 saw depressed case counts; 2022 is still partly affected, with recovery through 2023–2024. By-Land years here are 2022–2024; the national series (not in this mart) extends back to the pre-pandemic baseline.
- **Zensus 2022 series break:** population from 2022 onward uses the new census basis (earlier years use Zensus 2011), affecting per-100k comparisons across that boundary.
- **"Fälle" ≠ unique patients**, and cases are counted at the hospital's location, not the patient's residence — relevant for city-states and the Berlin–Brandenburg pair.
